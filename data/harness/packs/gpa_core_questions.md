# grant-proposal-assistant — four core questions

1. Central hypothesis — testable, specific, falsifiable. Prefer "X causes Y via Z".
2. Why important NOW — explicit knowledge gap and why timing is right.
3. What is innovative — specific new concept, method, application, or outcome.
4. Feasible and logical — independent aims, pitfalls + alternatives, team can deliver.

Fatal patterns: vague verbs (explore / characterize / determine), aim chains that collapse on one failure, missing controls, no power/sample-size rationale.
